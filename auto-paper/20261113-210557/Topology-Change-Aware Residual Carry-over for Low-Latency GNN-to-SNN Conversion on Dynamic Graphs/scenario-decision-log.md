## Candidate S1 — "A new lightweight spiking dynamic-graph architecture"

Rejected at **G4 (hard block)**. SpikeNet (AAAI 2023), Dy-SIGN (AAAI 2024), Lite-SDGN, SGNNBench and AMPLE already occupy dynamic-graph SNN architecture and efficiency diagnosis. A local predecessor audit `realsparse-v2-top-tier-close-v1.md` scored the related RealSparse-DyG idea **2.54/5, CLOSE** for exactly this overlap. Not advanced to G5–G8.

## Candidate S2 — "Spike-proxy sparse execution / energy Pareto on dynamic graphs"

Rejected at **G3 + G8**. The local machine has no neuromorphic hardware and no CUDA sparse-kernel path; a predecessor note states explicitly that energy claims are not supportable on a non-neuromorphic device. The historical 5x energy claim in `gnn2snn` was withdrawn. No falsifying mechanism remains once hardware energy is removed. Not advanced.

## Candidate S3 — "Dynamic-link-prediction robustness / negative-sampling correction"

Rejected at **G4 + G7**. The local predecessor `RiskSet-DyG` project already screened this family across ten candidate ideas (sampler invariance, cold start, memory reset, cache budget, motif, conformal, cross-policy adaptation) and closed all of them before download; its `p1-stoploss-review-v1.md` proved the core `−log q` law false and scored 42/100 with `pivot-with-rescue-route`. Conditional NCE (2018), *Correcting the LogQ Correction* (RecSys 2025) and CurNM (2024) cover the novelty. Not advanced.
