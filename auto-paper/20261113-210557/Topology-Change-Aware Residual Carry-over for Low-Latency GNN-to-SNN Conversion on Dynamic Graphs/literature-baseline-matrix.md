## Baseline-plan honesty

- Main scientific comparison is **conversion fidelity against the same ANN teacher**, not leaderboard MRR. Therefore the strongest *direct* baselines are conversion-method baselines (CRPI-style, SNNC-AP-light, DCGS coder, QCFS/FTBC), all of which are lightweight and runnable here.
- The dynamic-prediction SOTA methods are separated as `literature-context`. The manuscript will state that absolute predictive accuracy is reported as context and that no SOTA prediction claim is made.
- A matched **directly trained** spiking encoder of the same size is included as a parallel control, not as an oracle.
- Code availability, DOI, and venue metadata for every entry must be re-verified at P12/P14 before the bibliography is frozen. Several entries above are recorded from search-result text and local predecessor audits; they are **not yet citation-verified**.
